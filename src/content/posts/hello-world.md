Every blog starts with a first post, and this is mine.

I've been meaning to write more for a while. Not for an audience necessarily — more for the act of thinking something through by writing it down. There's something about putting words to an idea that forces it to become more precise.

This blog doesn't have a theme. It'll be whatever is on my mind — things I read, things I noticed, things I can't stop thinking about. Some of it will be half-formed. That's fine.

If you're reading this: hi.
